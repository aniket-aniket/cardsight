## Description (4000 characters max)
```
Cardsight identifies your trading cards from a photo and shows what they're worth today.

SNAP AND IDENTIFY
Take a photo or pick one from your library. Cardsight finds the card's edges, straightens it, reads the name and card number, and compares your photo with official card images to find the exact match, even telling apart reprints with identical artwork.

LIVE MARKET PRICES
See the current TCGplayer market price in USD, with the low-to-high listing range, the source, and when the price was last updated. Pick the printing you have (Normal, Holofoil, Reverse Holofoil and more) and the price updates to match.

HONEST CONFIDENCE
Every result shows a High, Medium or Low confidence label. Not sure it's right? Choose from other likely matches with one tap.

YOUR COLLECTION
Add cards with printing, condition and quantity. See your total collection value, refresh every price at once, and get a heads-up when you scan a card you already own.

PRICE TRACKING
Each price check is saved, so you can watch how a card's value, and your whole collection's value, changes over time.

CENTERING ESTIMATE
Measure a card's border centering from your photo and see how it compares with common grading centering limits before you send it off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Card recognition runs on your iPhone; your collection stays on your device.

Card data and prices are provided by TCGdex. Prices are estimates for near-mint cards and can vary by seller and condition. Centering is an estimate only and is not a grade.

Cardsight is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK or TCGplayer. Pokémon and all related names are trademarks of their respective owners.
```
