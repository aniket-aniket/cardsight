Cardsight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Cardsight ("the app") identifies trading cards from photos and shows their market value. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: we don't collect any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Cardsight has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We don't receive your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Photos and camera

The app uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, only to identify the card in it. Text recognition and image comparison run entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Network requests

To look up card details and prices, the app sends requests to the public TCGdex API. These requests contain only card information, such as a card name, set or number, and no personal data. TCGdex may receive standard technical information that any website receives, such as your IP address, under its own policies.

Data stored on your device

Your recent scans, collection and price history are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
